Director Log

A run of the pet with the director switched on. On the left is what the board reported every few seconds and at each moment: its mood and its own thought. On the right is what the director did with it. It either held back, asked the model and stayed quiet, or suggested something. Every suggestion is checked before it reaches the board, and “applied” means the board took it.
